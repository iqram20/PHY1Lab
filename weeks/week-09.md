## Pre-lab equipment and preparation

Confirm that all listed apparatus is present and working, locate scales/reference positions, inspect strings/fasteners, and write your measurement plan. Bring calculator, notebook, ruler, and graph paper as needed. Record apparatus-specific instrument resolution.

## Lab procedure — teaching summary

Measure projectile and catcher masses and the ballistic pendulum rise or capture angle according to apparatus; repeat launches. For the manual's range-fall check, secure the launcher at a table edge, mark impact area, measure drop height and horizontal range over repeated shots.

## Safety and common mistakes

Wear eye protection; clear projectile path and never aim at anyone. Collision conserves momentum approximately, not mechanical kinetic energy.
